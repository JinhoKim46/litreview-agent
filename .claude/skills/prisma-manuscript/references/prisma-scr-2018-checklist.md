# PRISMA-ScR 2018 Checklist Reference

This is a paraphrase of the PRISMA extension for Scoping Reviews (PRISMA-ScR) checklist (Tricco et al., 2018) -- the reporting standard for scoping reviews, replacing PRISMA 2020's synthesis/risk-of-bias items with charting/evidence-mapping items and PICO with PCC (Population/Concept/Context) framing. Use this as the authoritative list when drafting or auditing a scoping review's manuscript.

Source: Tricco AC, Lillie E, Zarin W, O'Brien KK, Colquhoun H, Levac D, et al. PRISMA Extension for Scoping Reviews (PRISMA-ScR): Checklist and Explanation. *Annals of Internal Medicine*. 2018;169(7):467-473. doi: 10.7326/M18-0850.

**License note:** unlike this repo's other PRISMA-family checklist files (PRISMA 2020, PRISMA-S, SWiM -- all published in BMJ/*Systematic Reviews*, both CC BY 4.0), this checklist is (c) 2018 American College of Physicians via *Annals of Internal Medicine*, a subscription journal. No CC BY or other open-reuse license was found for the checklist text itself (the official prisma-statement.org page states no explicit reuse license and directs reuse requests to the PRISMA Executive). The items below are therefore an original paraphrase of each item's substance, not a verbatim copy of the published wording -- checked against the official EQUATOR Network fillable-checklist form (equator-network.org) for accuracy of scope and substance, not copied from it.

## TITLE
| # | Item | Checklist |
|---|------|-----------|
| 1 | Title | State in the title that this is a scoping review. |

## ABSTRACT
| # | Item | Checklist |
|---|------|-----------|
| 2 | Structured summary | Give a structured abstract covering (as applicable): background, objectives, eligibility criteria, sources of evidence searched, charting methods, results, and conclusions relevant to the review's questions/objectives. |

## INTRODUCTION
| # | Item | Checklist |
|---|------|-----------|
| 3 | Rationale | Explain why this topic is being scoped rather than answered by a systematic review -- what is already known, and why a scoping approach fits the question/objective. |
| 4 | Objectives | State the review's questions/objectives explicitly, referencing their key elements (e.g. population, concept, and context -- PCC) or whatever elements were actually used to frame them. |

## METHODS
| # | Item | Checklist |
|---|------|-----------|
| 5 | Protocol and registration | State whether a protocol exists, where it can be found, and (if registered) the registration number. |
| 6 | Eligibility criteria | Specify the characteristics used as eligibility criteria for sources of evidence (e.g. years covered, language, publication status) and justify them. |
| 7 | Information sources | Describe every information source searched (e.g. databases with coverage dates, contacting authors for additional sources) and the date of the most recent search. |
| 8 | Search | Give the full electronic search strategy for at least one database, including any limits, in enough detail that it could be rerun. |
| 9 | Selection of sources of evidence | State how sources of evidence were screened and judged eligible for inclusion. |
| 10 | Data charting process | Describe how data were charted from included sources (e.g. piloted/calibrated forms, whether charting was done independently or in duplicate) and any process for confirming data with the original investigators. Note: this repo's manifest schema calls this stage "charting" rather than "extraction" for exactly this reason -- it is a distinct process from a systematic review's risk-of-bias-weighted extraction. |
| 11 | Data items | List and define every variable data were sought for, and any assumptions or simplifications made. |
| 12 | Critical appraisal of individual sources of evidence | If a critical appraisal of included sources was done (this is optional for a scoping review, unlike a systematic review), give the rationale, the method used, and how it fed into any synthesis. |
| 13 | Synthesis of results | Describe how the charted data were handled and summarized. |

## RESULTS
| # | Item | Checklist |
|---|------|-----------|
| 14 | Selection of sources of evidence | Report how many sources were screened, assessed for eligibility, and included, with reasons for exclusion at each stage -- ideally as a flow diagram. |
| 15 | Characteristics of sources of evidence | For each included source, present the charted characteristics and its citation. |
| 16 | Critical appraisal within sources of evidence | If item 12's appraisal was done, present its results here. |
| 17 | Results of individual sources of evidence | For each included source, present the charted data relevant to the review's questions/objectives. |
| 18 | Synthesis of results | Summarize and/or present the charted results as they relate to the review's questions/objectives. |

## DISCUSSION
| # | Item | Checklist |
|---|------|-----------|
| 19 | Summary of evidence | Summarize the main results (including the concepts, themes, and types of evidence found), tie them back to the review's questions/objectives, and note relevance to key audiences. |
| 20 | Limitations | Discuss the limitations of the scoping review process itself. |
| 21 | Conclusions | Give a general interpretation of the results relative to the review's questions/objectives, and any implications or next steps (e.g. whether a full systematic review is now warranted). |

## FUNDING
| # | Item | Checklist |
|---|------|-----------|
| 22 | Funding | Describe funding sources for both the included sources of evidence and for the scoping review itself, and the funders' role, if any. |
